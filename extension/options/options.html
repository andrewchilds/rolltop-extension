Privacy

Rolltop collects no usage data, in the browser or in this extension. Neither one reports to a server of ours, so we never see or have access to what you browse, in standard or private windows.

In the Rolltop browser, Firefox's telemetry, studies and crash reports are turned off. Your tabs, groups and archive are stored only in this profile. The one thing the extension can send anywhere is the AI sorting request described below, and only once you add an API key: it goes straight from this browser to Anthropic under your own key, and never to us.

AI sorting

With an Anthropic API key, Rolltop asks Claude to group tabs by what you are doing. Without one, tabs are grouped by website and nothing leaves this computer.

What is sent when a tab needs sorting: its title, its address without the query string or fragment, and the names of your existing groups with a few of their tab titles. The key is stored unencrypted in this Firefox profile.

claude-haiku-5-5 is the default. claude-opus-5-5 costs far more.

Private windows

Tabs in private windows are never sorted, with or without an API key. Nothing about them is sent to Anthropic, and they are not grouped by website either.

Rolltop keeps no record of them: they are left out of groups, the archive, the sorting log and workspace files. While open they show in the sidebar, ungrouped. There is no setting to change this.

Auto-tidy

A group you open again stays open until you next leave it alone this long.

Archived tabs are closed but stay searchable in the sidebar. Pinned tabs and tabs in pinned groups are never archived.

Workspace

A workspace file holds the open tabs of the workspace this window is showing, and the groups they are in. Export one here and import it in another browser to move those tabs there.

Archived tabs, these settings, private windows and pages that are not on the web are left out.

Adds to the workspace this window is showing: groups with the same name are merged, and a tab already open in its group is skipped. Tabs open in this window, unloaded until you visit them.

Backups

A backup holds every workspace: its groups, its tabs with their places, and its archive. Rolltop also keeps a snapshot from each of the last seven days it was used.

Restoring replaces every workspace with the backup's. No tab is closed or opened: open tabs go back to their places, tabs the backup does not know stay open, and its tabs that are not open go to the archive. What you had before is kept as a snapshot, so a restore can be undone. These settings and the API key are not in a backup.

Sorting log

The last 20 sorting runs: the tabs that needed a group, where each went and why, and the groups the model was shown. Kept on this computer only.
